## Sales & Operational Performance Dashboard

### Project Overview

This project delivers a multi-dimensional retail analytics solution engineered in Microsoft Excel to monitor macro-level sales health and supply chain operational efficiencies. The interactive ecosystem normalizes fragmented commercial data to track profitability, analyze consumer payment preferences, isolate regional demand, and map delivery status metrics across dynamic product categories like Beauty, Clothing, Electronics, and Home Decor.

### Tools & Technologies

Platform: Microsoft Excel

Analytical Processing: Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, Advanced Logical Array Formulas

Interactivity Layout: Multi-slicer parameter controls (Sales Channel, Payment Mode, Delivery Status, Region, and Product Category)

### Key Business Insights

* Macro Financial Totals: Consolidated performance records Total Sales of 5,604,598.96 yielding a Total Profit of 1,312,009.30.

* Geographic Consolidation: Market demand is heavily centralized within a single territory, with the East Region capturing 100% of recorded sales (56,04,599), while Central, North, South, and West channels show zero baseline activity.

* Balanced Channels & Payments: The transaction infrastructure is remarkably uniform:

* Channels: Revenue splits perfectly at an exact 50% Online and 50% Offline equilibrium.

* Payments: Market preference shows zero friction points across transaction types, split evenly among Card (25%), Wallet (25%), COD (24%), and UPI (26%).

* Temporal Volatility: Sales peak early in the fiscal cycle, with January leading at 13.86%, followed closely by March (12.63%) and February (12.29%), before dropping to a tight, sustained valley ranging from 5.91% to 7.79% for the remaining three quarters of the year.

* Supply Chain and Logistical Friction: A deep dive into delivery fulfillment reveals severe operational bottlenecks:

* Successful Delivery: Only 1,389,004.79 worth of stock successfully reached customers.

* In-Transit Risk: 1,567,078.87 remains logistically locked in limbo.

* Shrinkage & Returns: Cancelled orders (1,324,920.18) and Returned products (1,323,595.12) match successful deliveries virtually 1:1, eroding bottom-line profit margins.

### Strategic Recommendations

Insight: Cancelled and returned orders collectively outnumber successfully delivered goods, heavily limiting net revenue.

* Action: Launch an immediate quality control audit on logistics providers and introduce address verification protocols to lower the high rates of delivery cancellation and returns.

Insight: Sales volumes face an aggressive collapse following Q1, plummeting from 13.86% in January to a low of 5.91% by April.

* Action: Build out mid-year promotional campaigns, seasonal product bundles, and targeted marketing pushes across Q2 and Q3 to counter post-Q1 sales drops.

Insight: The East Region completely dominates the sales landscape with 100% territory allocation.

* Action: Establish dedicated distribution hubs within the East to support this core market, while performing localized field research in the Central, North, and West regions to diagnose zero-revenue barriers.

Insight: Transaction channels and payment modes show an exact, uniform split across online/offline ecosystems and digital payment types.

* Action: Avoid shifting resources toward a single payment gate. Maintain a broad, omni-channel operational footprint to support this highly diverse consumer base.
